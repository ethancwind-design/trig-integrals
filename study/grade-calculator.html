The final exam also sets the overall grade distribution, so this raw score is not the same as your letter grade.

The method

  1. Each quiz is a score out of 40. Sort the six scores from lowest to highest.
  2. Give each rank a weight: lowest $0\%$, then $4\%$, $7\%$, $10\%$, $13\%$, $16\%$ (the weights add up to $50\%$).
  3. A quiz is worth $\text{weight}\times\dfrac{\text{score}}{40}$ points.
  4. The rest is $33\%\times\text{final}+5\%\times\text{prep quizzes}+12\%\times\text{recitation}$, with each of those as a percent out of 100.
  5. Add everything up. Raw score $=\text{quiz points}+\text{final}+\text{prep}+\text{recitation}$.

The weights come from the course calculator this page is based on. Check them against your syllabus before you rely on the number.
