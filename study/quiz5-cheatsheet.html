1 · Arc length

\(L=\displaystyle\int_a^b\sqrt{1+\left(\tfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^2}\,dx\)   (curve given as \(y=f(x)\))
\(L=\displaystyle\int_c^d\sqrt{1+\left(\tfrac{dx}{dy}\right)^2}\,dy\)   (curve given as \(x=g(y)\))
\(L=\displaystyle\int_\alpha^\beta\sqrt{\left(\tfrac{dx}{dt}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{dy}{dt}\right)^2}\,dt\)   (parametric)

Key move: differentiate → square → add 1 → find the perfect square → √ cancels.

4 · Centroids and center of mass

Top \(f\), bottom \(g\), \(a\le x\le b\), density \(\rho(x)\) (centroid: \(\rho=1\), \(m=A\)):
\(m=\int_a^b\rho\,(f-g)\,dx\)    \(M_y=\int_a^b x\,\rho\,(f-g)\,dx\) → \(\bar x=\frac{M_y}m\)
\(M_x=\tfrac12\int_a^b\rho\,(f^2-g^2)\,dx\) → \(\bar y=\frac{M_x}m\)  \(f^2-g^2\), not \((f-g)^2\)
Rod: \(\bar x=\frac{\int x\rho\,dx}{\int\rho\,dx}\). Point masses: \(\bar x=\frac{\sum m_ix_i}{\sum m_i}\).
Solid about x-axis, \(\rho(x)\): \(\bar x=\frac{\int x\rho\,\pi(R^2-r^2)dx}{\int\rho\,\pi(R^2-r^2)dx}\).

5 · Probability density functions

Valid pdf: \(f(x)\ge0\) and \(\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,dx=1\).   \(P(a\le X\le b)=\displaystyle\int_a^bf(x)\,dx\), \(P(X=a)=0\).
Mean \(\mu=\displaystyle\int x\,f(x)\,dx\).   Median \(m\): \(\displaystyle\int_{\text{left end}}^{m}f(x)\,dx=\tfrac12\).
\(\sigma^2=\displaystyle\int x^2f(x)\,dx-\mu^2\) (subtract \(\mu^2\)!)   \(\sigma=\sqrt{\sigma^2}\).
CDF \(F(x)=P(X\le x)=\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^xf(t)\,dt\),   \(f=F'\). \(P(X>a)=1-F(a)\). Integrate only over the support.

7 · Integration moves (from Weeks 7–9)

u-sub: change the bounds, or substitute back. If an \(x\) is left over, write it in \(u\) (\(x=u-1\)).
Parts: \(\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du\) (u = log, then polynomial).
\(\int xe^{-x}dx=-xe^{-x}-e^{-x}\)   \(\int\ln x\,dx=x\ln x-x\)
\(\int x\ln x\,dx=\tfrac{x^2}2\ln x-\tfrac{x^2}4\)   \(\int(\ln x)^2dx=x(\ln x)^2-2x\ln x+2x\)
Trig sub results:
\(\int\frac{dx}{(x^2+a^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{x}{a^2\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}\)
\(\int\frac{dx}{(a^2-x^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{x}{a^2\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}\)
\(\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}=\arcsin\frac xa\)    \(\int\frac{dx}{a^2+x^2}=\frac1a\arctan\frac xa\)
Partial fractions: \(\frac1{x^2-a^2}=\frac1{2a}\big[\frac1{x-a}-\frac1{x+a}\big]\)
Trig: \(\sin^2x=\tfrac{1-\cos2x}2\), \(\cos^2x=\tfrac{1+\cos2x}2\), \(1+\tan^2=\sec^2\), \(\int\sec x\,dx=\ln|\sec x+\tan x|\), \(\cosh^2-\sinh^2=1\)
Improper: \(\int_1^\infty x^{-p}dx=\tfrac1{p-1}\) (\(p>1\)); write \(\lim_{b\to\infty}\), \(e^{-b}\to0\).

8 · Answer shapes (check yourself)

  • Arc lengths here come out clean (6, \(\tfrac{14}3\), \(\tfrac{21}{16}\), 147) or as \(\ln(\dots)\). A messy root means you missed the perfect square.
  • Surface area has a π and is positive.
  • Centroid sits on every symmetry line; semicircle \(\bar y\approx0.42r\)
  • \(f_{avg}\) is between the min and max of \(f\).
  • Probabilities are in \([0,1]\); \(\mu\) and \(m\) are inside the support; \(\sigma^2>0\); exponential median \(<\) mean.

9 · Workflow (every problem)

  1. Pick the tool (§0).
  2. Sketch: curve or region, axis, bounds (solve for intersections).
  3. Pick \(dx\), \(dy\) or \(dt\) from how the curve is given.
  4. Write the full integral, with bounds and \(dx\), before simplifying.
  5. Simplify: perfect square, cancel the radius, use symmetry.
  6. Integrate (§7); change the bounds with \(u\).
  7. Divide (average, centroid) or set equal to 1 or ½ (pdf, median).
  8. Check against §8.

10 · Common mistakes

  • Surface area: \(2\pi r\,dL\), not \(2\pi r\,dx\).
  • Variance: subtract \(\mu^2\).
  • \(M_x=\tfrac12\int(f^2-g^2)\), not \(\tfrac12\int(f-g)^2\).
  • Forgetting to change the bounds after a substitution.
  • Leaving \(y\) in a \(dx\) integral: the radius must be in the integration variable.
  • \(\int u^{-2}du=-\tfrac1u\) (minus sign).
  • Mixing up \(M_y\to\bar x\) and \(M_x\to\bar y\).
  • Median: set the integral of \(f\) equal to ½, not \(f\) itself.
  • Integrating a pdf outside where it is nonzero.
  • MVT: listing only one \(c\) when there are two.
